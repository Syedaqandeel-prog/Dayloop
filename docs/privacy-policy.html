Dayloop Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Innovexa Technologies

Who we are

Dayloop is a habit and routine app made by Innovexa Technologies. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the things you enter so it can work: your name, profile photo, habits, completed habits, mood check-ins, water count, journal lines, Self-Care Day plans, badges, challenges and your settings. All of this is stored only on your device. Dayloop has no account system, and we do not receive or store this information on any server.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, email address or device identifiers. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a profile photo, the app asks to use your camera or photo library. The photo you pick is resized and saved only on your device, and you can remove it at any time from your profile. The app never accesses your camera or photos without your action.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in the app from Profile → Settings → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your phone. Because we do not store your data, there is no account to delete on our side.

Children

Dayloop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app with a new effective date.
